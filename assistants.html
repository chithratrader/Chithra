What an assistant does for you

Answers common questions

Opening hours, prices, location, policies and ordering. The questions your team answers every day.

Stays on message

It answers only from the information you provide, so it never invents prices or promises.

Passes things to you

When a question needs a person, it points customers to your phone number or contact options.

Takes messages and booking requests Coming soon

Collects a customer's details and sends the request to you by email, so you can confirm it yourself.

How it works

  1. Learn your business

    We gather your common questions, prices and policies, ideally from real customer messages.

  2. Build the assistant

    Your information becomes a knowledge file, with a tone and rules that suit your brand.

  3. Test with real questions

    We try the questions your customers actually ask, and fix any answer that isn't right.

  4. Launch on your website

    A chat bubble appears in the corner of your site. No new app for customers to install.

  5. Keep improving

    We review the questions it couldn't answer and add them to the knowledge file.
